Phase 1: Tensors and setup
Tensors: is like a box where it stores numbers , and during learniing phase or model training, 
an optimizer updates the numbers using gradients to minimize the loss or mistakes. 

AUTOGRAD AND TRAINING
Autograd is PyTorch's automatic differentiation engine.

It calculates how much each learnable parameter contributes to the change in the loss (error).

import torch
x = torch.tensor(2.0) --> its a normal tensor
y = torch.tensor(3.0, requires_grad=True) --> here requires_grad tells pytorch to keep the track of 
variable cux it may need to calculate its gradient

y_true = torch.tensor(10.0) --> its actual number model needs to predict close to 10
pred = x * y
loss = (pred - y_true)**2 --> mean squared error

print(loss)
loss.backward() -->how much loss changes when a particular variable changes slighlty
print(y.grad) --> its stores the gradiant .

TRAINING LOOP
A training loop is a process where a model repeatedly makes predictions, 
calculates the loss, and uses gradients to update its learnable parameters.

This process repeats for multiple epochs until the model learns the patterns and minimizes the prediction error.
